## PART 6 — IMMIGRATION CV (full spec)

**Sections, in order:**
1. Header — full legal name exactly as on passport; no photo, no tagline
2. Professional summary — 3–5 lines framing you in terms of the endeavor
3. Education — all institutions, full detail, month/year dates, thesis/capstones
4. Research experience — READS Lab (above finance for this petition)
5. Professional experience — every role, month/year dates, quantified bullets; banking framed as transferable capability
6. Projects & tools — names, launch dates, function, tech, NUMBERS
7. Ventures — sameerlearn, finanai: dates, mission, traction
8. Publications — full academic citations + DOI + Scholar link
9. Presentations & talks (grow this year)
10. Honors & awards (modest)
11. Certifications & training
12. Teaching & mentoring (keep — supports education narrative)
13. Peer review & judging (add when earned)
14. Media & coverage (add when earned)
15. Memberships
16. Technical skills
17. Languages

**Rules:**
- A. 3–8 pages; completeness beats brevity
- B. **Consistency is sacred** — every date/title must match transcripts, I-983, EADs, letters, I-140 (mismatch = RFE trigger)
- C. **Verifiable only** — misrepresentation carries permanent immigration consequences; "$100M" only if documentable
- D. Quantify relentlessly
- E. Architecture mirrors the endeavor (research/projects forward, finance supporting)
- F. No fluff adjectives
- G. Month/year precision on everything (fix the "2024–2024" LinkedIn vagueness)
- H. Living document — version it, keep synced with evidence file

## PART 11 — RULES & WARNINGS (read monthly)

1. **Eligibility is SETTLED.** Advanced degree ✓. No licenses, certifications, EB-1 criteria, or "exceptional ability" items needed — ever. Stop shopping eligibility.
2. **All three prongs must each pass.** No averaging, no mixing. One endeavor, plural evidence.
3. **Letters are claims; exhibits are evidence.** Every letter claim needs a document behind it.
4. **EAD approvals ≠ petition strength.** Status is strong; the petition is built, not assumed.
5. **GPA, honors, and credentials are threshold items, not arguments.** The case is impact documentation.
6. **Never embellish.** Immigration misrepresentation = permanent bars. Verifiable only, everywhere.
7. **Consistency across every document.** CV ↔ transcripts ↔ I-983 ↔ letters ↔ forms.
8. **20 home-campus trial users = a seed, not adoption.** Outside-Harvard reach is the conversion that matters.
9. **Finance is supporting cast.** It proves you can execute and scale — it is never the endeavor.
10. **Institutionalize doubt.** No attorney means YOU build the review process (clinic, limited-scope review, red-team).
